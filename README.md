# CropSense

CropSense is a Java-based agricultural management system designed to help farmers, agricultural organizations, and authorities manage and analyze important crop-related information.

The system focuses on tracking **crop production, surplus, exports, market demand, and supply trends**. By organizing this information in one system, CropSense can help users better understand agricultural conditions and make more informed decisions about production, distribution, and planning.

## Overview

Agriculture generates a large amount of data related to crop production, market demand, surplus, exports, and supply. Managing this information effectively can help reduce waste, identify opportunities, and support better planning.

CropSense provides a system for organizing and managing this information. Users can keep track of crop production and surplus, monitor exportable products, and analyze market demand and supply to support agricultural decision-making.

## Objectives

The main objectives of CropSense are to:

* Track and manage crop production information.
* Monitor available crop surplus.
* Identify products that can be exported.
* Track market demand and supply.
* Organize agricultural data in a structured system.
* Support better agricultural planning and decision-making.
* Reduce potential losses caused by poor production and market planning.

## Features

### Crop Production Management

Users can record and manage information about different crops and their production levels.

* Add crop production records.
* View production information.
* Track production quantities.
* Manage information for different crops.

### Surplus Tracking

CropSense allows users to keep track of agricultural products that remain after satisfying current requirements.

* Record surplus production.
* Monitor available surplus.
* Identify crops with excess production.
* Use surplus information for further planning.

### Export Management

The system can help identify and manage information about agricultural products that are suitable for export.

* Track exportable crops.
* Manage export-related information.
* Monitor quantities available for export.
* Use production and surplus data to support export planning.

### Market Demand Analysis

Understanding market demand is important for agricultural planning. CropSense provides a way to organize market-related information.

* Track demand for different crops.
* Compare supply and demand.
* Identify crops with high or low demand.
* Support production planning based on market needs.

### Decision Support

The information managed by CropSense can help users make better decisions regarding:

* What crops to produce.
* How much to produce.
* How to manage surplus.
* Which products may be suitable for export.
* How production can respond to market demand.

## How It Works

The basic workflow of CropSense can be represented as:

```text
Crop Production
       ↓
Production Data
       ↓
Surplus & Supply Tracking
       ↓
Market Demand Analysis
       ↓
Export Opportunities
       ↓
Agricultural Decision Making
```

The system collects agricultural information and organizes it so that users can understand production, supply, surplus, and demand more easily.

## Why CropSense?

CropSense is designed around a simple idea: **better agricultural data can lead to better agricultural decisions**.

By bringing production, surplus, exports, supply, and market demand into one system, CropSense aims to provide a clearer picture of agricultural activity and help users make decisions based on organized information rather than assumptions.
